Lasagna

Description

This is a traditional italian meal.

it's easy and fast to make

Ingredients

  • Pound sweet italian sausage
  • Pound ground beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Cheese

Steps to follow to make Lasagna

  1. Make the meat sauce
  2. Cook the noodles
  3. Layer the lasagna
  4. Bake for around 45minutes
  5. Let it rest a bit before serving
